Privacy Policy

Tapify Sales Crm · Last updated: 8 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tapify Sales Crm (the “App”, package com.kbamnote.salesTapify) collects, uses, and protects information. The App is an internal workforce-management tool provided to employees of our organization for sales, attendance, and field-activity management. It is not intended for use by the general public.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & profile information: name, email, phone number, role, designation, department, employee ID, and an optional profile photo.
  • Location data: your device’s GPS location, used for attendance punch-in/out verification and for sharing your work location with your manager (see Section 3).
  • Camera images: selfies captured during attendance punch-in/out to verify identity, and photos you choose to upload (e.g., profile picture).
  • Work data you enter: leads, clients, field visits, meetings, closed deals, targets, and chat messages with your team.
  • Device & technical data: a push-notification token used to deliver notifications to your device.

2. Background Location

The App collects location data to enable live work-location sharing with your manager even when the App is closed or not in use.
  • Background location is collected only while you are punched in for your shift. It begins when you punch in and stops automatically when you punch out.
  • While location sharing is active, an ongoing notification is displayed on your device.
  • The purpose is to allow your manager to confirm that field staff are present in their assigned territory during working hours.
  • Background location is only enabled after you are shown an in-app disclosure and grant the “Allow all the time” location permission. You may revoke this at any time in your device settings; attendance can still be used without it.

3. How We Use Information

  • To verify attendance (location + selfie) and record working hours.
  • To share your live work location with your managers during working hours.
  • To manage sales activity: leads, clients, visits, deals, and targets.
  • To enable team chat and to send you notifications (e.g., deal closed, new message, assigned lead).
  • To display performance dashboards to you and your managers.

4. How Information Is Shared

  • Within your organization: your data is visible to your managers, administrators, and HR as required for workforce management.
  • Service providers: images may be stored using a cloud media provider (Cloudinary), and push notifications are delivered via Expo/Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. These providers process data only to provide their service.
  • We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with third parties for advertising.
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as you are an employee using the App and as needed for legitimate business and legal purposes. Location records are retained only as long as necessary for attendance and field-verification purposes.

6. Security

Access to the App requires authentication. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access is restricted based on your role within the organization. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. Your Choices

  • You can decline or revoke the background-location permission at any time in your device settings; core attendance features remain available.
  • You may update your profile details and photo within the App.
  • To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, contact us using the details below. As this is an employer-provided tool, some records may be retained as required for employment and legal purposes.

8. Children

The App is intended for employees and is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App or by your organization. The “Last updated” date above reflects the latest revision.
